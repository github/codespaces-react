Guide To A Vampire Lifestyle

Fundamentals

Goal Setting

to get started, consider if you want to drink more or less blood depending on your ambitions. its important to be realistic and understand that a powerful LIFEFORCE cannot be rushed

Nutrition

quality of blood is ABSOLUTE and will determine everything from your hunts and recovery to how you look and feel 6 months from now. nutritious feasting is absolutely NON NEGOTIABLE

Hunting

your going to want to plan your hunts around your daytime persona, hunting atleast 3 times a week with rest in between. you must find the right balance between your hunts and rest as not to exaust your AURA

Mending

adequate rest and feasting is essential to growing your LIFEFORCE and MANA. without a full nights rest and proper devouring you only delay or outright hinder your LIFESPAN from evolving.
